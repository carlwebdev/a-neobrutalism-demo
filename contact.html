Frequently asked chaos

How soon can we start?

Discovery calls can happen within two days. Production kickoffs typically land two weeks after contract sign-off.

Do you work with startups?

Absolutely. We love early-stage weirdos. Expect a structured process, transparent estimates, and plenty of prototypes.

Can you handle physical installations?

Yes. Our Berlin crew leads fabrication, while the NYC team manages logistics. We coordinate vendors, permits, and on-site activation.

What about retainers?

We offer rolling retainers for product design, content systems, and growth experimentation. Ask in your brief and we will send the options.
