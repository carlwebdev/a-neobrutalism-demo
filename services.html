How it happens

The brutalist playbook

Structured chaos, weekly check-ins, and artifacts you can actually use. Here’s how we guide every engagement.

  1. Phase 01

    Immersion brief

    We tear apart the brief together, audit what exists, and build a north-star statement the entire crew can rally around.

  2. Phase 02

    Concept lab

    Rapid experiments, mood tests, and prototype spikes. You get tangible options, not vague vibes.

  3. Phase 03

    Build + refine

    We formalize the chosen direction, engineer the assets or product, and stress test with real users when it matters.

  4. Phase 04

    Launch care

    Activation runbooks, training, and post-launch analytics ensure the momentum keeps swinging in your favor.
